How Sandblasting Works for Non-Slip Sealing

To achieve a surface that’s resistant to slipping, you’ll want to know how sandblasting operates for this purpose. Sandblasting involves high-pressure air and abrasive material, like sand, to remove dirt, rust, or old coatings from surfaces.

When it comes to non-slip sealing, sandblasting creates a rough texture on the surface being treated. This texture enhances its grip on shoes or tires, ensuring any sealant applied will adhere more effectively. The result is a safer walking or driving area.

Sandblasting prepares surfaces for non-slip sealing by removing any contaminants or debris that could hinder adhesion. The rough texture created by sandblasting provides a better bond for sealants and coatings than a smooth surface would.
