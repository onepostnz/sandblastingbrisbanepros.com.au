What is Sandblasting and How Does it Work?

Sandblasting involves the use of compressed air, water, or steam to propel abrasive particles at high speeds onto a surface to remove unwanted materials. The abrasives used can include anything from sand, glass beads, walnut shells, aluminum oxide, and more.

The force of these particles hitting the surface removes rust, paint, dirt, and other contaminants, leaving behind a clean smooth surface. This process is used in various industries, including automotive repair, construction, manufacturing, and even art restoration.
