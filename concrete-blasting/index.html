Removing Old Coatings and Contaminants

If you want to ensure a clean slate for future projects, it’s important to remove old coatings and contaminants from your concrete surfaces. Our sandblasting Brisbane pros have the advanced techniques and equipment needed to effectively remove these unwanted substances.

We use a combination of abrasive blasting materials and high-pressure water to strip away any existing coatings, paints, or stains, as well as any rust, oil, or other contaminants that may be present. By removing these unwanted substances from your concrete surface, we can prepare it for the next step of our concrete blasting service: exposing aggregate with blasting.

This process involves using our powerful equipment to blast away the top layer of cement paste on your surface, revealing the underlying aggregate beneath. Exposing this aggregate gives your surface an attractive textured finish that is also slip-resistant – making it ideal for areas such as pool decks or driveways where safety is a concern.

Exposing Aggregate with Blasting

To achieve a textured and slip-resistant finish on your concrete surface, consider exposing the aggregate with our advanced blasting techniques. Our team of sandblasting Brisbane pros can help you achieve the desired look for your space by removing the top layer of cement paste to reveal the natural stone underneath.

Here are 4 reasons why you should choose our blasting service to expose aggregate:

1. Enhance durability: Exposing the aggregate makes your concrete surface more durable as it increases its resistance to wear and tear, making it ideal for high-traffic areas like walkways, driveways, and patios.

2. Create a unique aesthetic: The exposed aggregate creates a beautiful aesthetic that is unique to each project. With various stones in different shapes and sizes, no two surfaces will ever be identical.

3. Improve safety: By exposing the aggregate, we create a slip-resistant surface that improves safety for pedestrians and vehicles alike.

4. Low-maintenance: Exposed aggregate requires minimal maintenance as dirt and stains are less visible than on smooth surfaces. This makes it an excellent choice for commercial properties with high foot traffic where cleaning can become time-consuming if not done frequently enough.
